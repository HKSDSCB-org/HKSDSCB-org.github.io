What embryos teach us about cancer and other diseases

Mok Hing-Yiu Distinguished Visiting Professorship Public Lecture · Hosted by Prof. Kathryn Cheah

Molecular programs that control embryonic development can be reactivated in disease. This lecture explores how cell plasticity drives cancer metastasis — and what embryos can teach us about preventing and treating disease.
