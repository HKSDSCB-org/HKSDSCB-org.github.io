Hong Kong Society for Developmental and Stem Cell Biology

Linking Hong Kong Developmental and Stem Cell Biologists with the international community

Mission

  • ​To foster the advancement of developmental biology, genetics and genomics and its relevance to regenerative medicine and human congenital birth defects;
​
  • To provide a research network and forum for scientists and clinicians in Hong Kong for exchanging ideas, resources, collaborations and information on the latest scientific and technological advances in the field;
​
  • ​To facilitate interactions with other researchers and societies internationally; and
​
  • ​​To provide a focus for the promotion of public awareness and understanding of key issues and the importance of the field.
